隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date：2026-10-10

一、概述

本应用「手机警报器」（以下称「本应用」）由「我爱玩软件」开发并发布。我们非常重视你的隐私。本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。

二、我们收集的信息

本应用不收集、不存储、不上传任何个人信息。具体来说：

  • 不需要注册账号
  • 不收集设备标识符
  • 不收集位置信息
  • 不含广告 SDK、统计 SDK 或任何第三方追踪组件

三、麦克风权限

本应用在「扩音喊话」功能中会申请麦克风权限，用于：

  • 录制你的语音
  • 将录制内容保存在你的设备本地，用于回放

录制的音频文件仅保存在你的设备上，不会上传到任何服务器，也不会被开发者或任何第三方获取。你可以随时在 iOS「设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 麦克风」中撤销此权限。

四、本地存储

本应用使用浏览器 localStorage 保存你的偏好设置（如语言、音效参数）。这些数据仅存储在你的设备上，卸载 App 即会清除。

五、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不收集任何年龄相关信息。

六、政策更新

如本政策有变更，我们会在本页面更新「生效日期」，并可能通过 App 更新说明告知你。

七、联系方式

如有任何隐私相关问题，请联系邮箱：hengjixu@qq.com

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-10

1. Overview

This app "Mobile Siren" (the "App") is developed and published by "我爱玩软件" ("we", "us"). We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

2. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account registration required
  • No device identifiers collected
  • No location data collected
  • No advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or third-party tracking components

3. Microphone Permission

The App requests microphone permission for the "PA Hailer" feature to:

  • Record your voice
  • Store the recording locally on your device for playback

Recorded audio is stored only on your device and is never uploaded to any server. Neither the developer nor any third party can access it. You can revoke this permission anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

4. Local Storage

The App uses browser localStorage to save your preferences (language, audio settings). This data is stored only on your device and is cleared when the App is uninstalled.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect any age-related information.

6. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the "Effective date" on this page and may notify you through the App update notes.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact: hengjixu@qq.com
